## Method, and the two things that made the difference

**17 read-only lenses** over the tree, each required to produce findings with a pasted
reproduction. Then **one fresh skeptic per finding**, instructed to REFUTE it and to default to
refuted when uncertain. 135 agents, ~5,000 tool uses.

    207 findings — 24 blocking, 83 major, 71 minor, 29 opportunity
    118 adversarially verified — 94 CONFIRMED, 17 downgraded, 7 REFUTED
    every BLOCKING finding was verified

**The adversarial pass earned its cost and is the part to keep.** It refuted 7 outright — including
one the lead had reached independently — downgraded 17, and in several cases rejected the auditor's
proposed fix and supplied a better one; in one it *proved* a proposed fix ineffective against its
own reproduction. Two skeptics disagreed about the same function, and that fork was handed to the
builder to settle by measurement rather than by argument.

**Two lenses account for most of the 207**, and they are the ones to reach for first: *a guard
answering its undecidable case with the passing value*, and *a decision reading state a restart
empties* — the latter found by asking, of every `Map`, `Set`, class field and closure in `run/`,
`server/` and `resources/`: what decision reads this, and what does it do when a restart hands it
back empty? Then looking where the existing tests do not — at a CHILD run.

The full register, the per-lens reports and every verdict are `.agent/full-audit-2026-09-02/`
(gitignored working state).

## The reproductions `CLAUDE.md` §3 refers to

### Property 3 was faked, and this was the headline

`extractSignals` read S1 — "the only signal a model cannot argue with" — out of the CANDIDATE'S OWN
graph: any `evaluator` step whose commit carried `{pass:true}` and made no model call. A candidate
owns its graph, so it owned S1, the outcome, the promotion ceiling and the ground-truth condition.

1. A candidate that **deletes the work node** and returns `{pass:true}` promoted through
   `loom promote --against-cohort` at paired mean Δscore **+0.4000 over 30 pairs**.
2. A candidate whose only change was **swapping its own grader** promoted through
   `loom promote --suite` with **all thirteen** checks green (thirteen was that day's count).
3. **Corpus poisoning through freeze**: publish (2), and `loom score` reads S1 from the rigged
   grader, `isGolden` marks garbage golden, and the next `loom suite freeze` pins it as must-pass
   ground truth — 20 of 30 golden, 10 must-pass cases pinning a dropped item.

`freezeSuite` excludes grader-written channels from the pin by construction, so the one node the
exam could not see was the one a candidate could rewrite. Three earlier attempts to pin a verifier
are at `aabdc63`; the shape that defeated all three is that the candidate owns both sides of any
channel the graph produces. **Closed by the operator-attested exam** — designed in
`docs/design-property3-2026-09-05.md`, merged at `ec2ad88`, verified on `loom` at `ce9e7b4`; both
reproductions now refuse at the `--against-cohort` door and the honest candidate promotes at the
same +0.4000 that used to certify the fake.

### Journal violations seven and eight

Both in the blind spot the existing restart test does not look at — **a delegated child run**.
`grantBound` (the capability allowlist a parent narrows for its child) and the child's dollar slice
lived only as arguments to `#contextFor`, recorded in no journal the CHILD's own fold could read.
A `pay.charge` refused `E_CAP_DENIED` **charged after a restart**; a child bounded to $0.01
**succeeded and spent**. Both closed; `test/run/lane-a-child-bounds-survive-restart.test.ts` and
`test/run/rewind-applies-the-parents-bound.test.ts` are the pins.

## One correction that outlived the audit

**A per-task ordering of served channel state is NOT a missing journal fact**, and any row that
proposes one as a fix is mis-scoped. The served view is not *stored* but it is fully
*reconstructible*: `foldRun(store.read(runId, 1, seqOf(task.leased)))` then
`viewFor(p, spec.channels, task.branch, observedChannels(node))` returns **byte-identically** what
the body was handed, fan-out bindings and overwritten channels included; `StateStore.read` already
takes an end bound. What is missing is that **nothing asks** — no `foldRun` call site answers "what
was task X served", and `cli.ts` has no `--at-seq`. A read-model affordance **outside** the kernel,
needing no new durable event.
