## What does not

### Property 3 is faked, and this is the headline
A candidate that **deletes the work node** and returns `{pass:true}` promotes at paired mean
Δscore **+0.4000 over 30 pairs**, all eight checks green. A candidate whose only change is swapping
its own grader promotes with **all thirteen** checks green. S1 — "the only signal a model cannot
argue with" — is read out of the candidate's own graph, and a candidate owns its graph.
`freezeSuite` excludes grader-written channels from the pin by construction, so the one node the
exam cannot see is the one a candidate may rewrite.

### The journal was authoritative seven and eight times less than believed
Both in the same blind spot the existing restart test does not look at — **a delegated child run**.
`grantBound` and the child's dollar slice lived only as arguments. A `pay.charge` refused
`E_CAP_DENIED` **charges after a restart**; a child bounded to $0.01 **succeeds and spends**.

### Four live prompt-injection paths to an irreversible action
Each driven through the real `Engine` with a real charge counter and a human de-escalation
journaled before any untrusted byte existed: `gates=0`, charge made.

### A fan-out is quadratic in its own branch count
200 branches 42 ms; 3,200 branches 10,717 ms — 16× the width for 255× the time. A CPU profile puts
**47.5%** in `projection.ts`'s `freeze` (a full container copy per snapshot, ~2 per task) and 24.2%
in four `Object.values(p.tasks)` scans per commit. Separately, `compileOrThrow` runs `indexGraph`
**twice** and throws one transitive closure away — a patched copy took a 1,500-node compile from
490 ms to 238 ms with no semantic change.

### One denial of service on the runtime itself
`fs.grep` builds a `RegExp` from **model-authored text** and runs it synchronously: **86 seconds of
blocked event loop**, uninterruptible by any deadline, `AbortSignal` or result cap.

### And the guards that protect the guards
`check-kernel.mjs`'s ledger is resettable three ways (see CLAUDE.md §1, corrected).
`check-surface.mjs` is executed by **no test at all** — neuter it and both the suite and CI stay
green while the pin is wrong. Two tests pass with the guard they were written for deleted.

## Two corrections to the previous handoff

1. **`cli/cli.test.ts` does not swallow its reporter.** Measured 38/38.
2. **A per-task ordering of served channel state is NOT a missing journal fact**, and the previous
   handoff called it "the highest-leverage single change in the tree". The served view is not
   *stored* but it is fully *reconstructible*:
   `foldRun(store.read(runId, 1, seqOf(task.leased)))` then
   `viewFor(p, spec.channels, task.branch, observedChannels(node))` returns **byte-identically**
   what the body was handed, including per-item fan-out bindings and channels a later task
   overwrote. `StateStore.read` already takes an end bound.
   What is missing is that **nothing asks**: `foldRun` has five call sites and none answers "what
   was task X served", and `cli.ts` has no `--at-seq`. That is a read-model affordance **outside**
   the kernel — cheaper than the previous handoff implied, and needing no new durable event.
   A.29 and DESIGN item 20 should be re-scoped accordingly.
