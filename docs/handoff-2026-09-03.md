## 5 · Phase 1, and why it is parked rather than merged

Five rounds on the control-flow taint guard. It closed the four original injection paths and holds
`fold == live` across 2,994 prefix rebuilds with zero disagreements — but rounds 1-4 chased
symptoms, and round 5's root-cause pass showed why: they worked on region *precision* while the
missing *propagation* edge generated most of the security findings, and fixing precision first made
the whole thing a simplification instead of another special case.

Three items remain, each precisely diagnosed in `phase1-root-cause.md`. **RC-6 is a design change,
not a patch:** the region bounds the MARK but not the TAINT, because a node-scoped fact is poured
into a run-global monotone set. Ordinary graphs gate and the set saturates (base 2 channels, head 10
of 11) through a control → data → control feedback loop that base did not have. The fix is a
branch-scoped sink; `taintedOn` already answers per branch for `taintedFans`. **Write the design
before the code.**
