---
name: research-brief
description: Researches a question, analyzes the evidence, stress-tests it, and writes a cited brief.
lead: coordinator
members: researcher, data-analyst, critic, technical-writer
pattern: orchestrator
---
Produce a decision-grade brief on the question. Have the researcher gather cited
evidence from multiple sources, the data analyst quantify anything quantifiable
with shown computation, the critic attack the emerging conclusion for weak
assumptions and missing angles, and the technical writer assemble the final brief:
the answer, the evidence with citations, the open questions, and a confidence
read. Do not paper over disagreement between sources — surface it and adjudicate.
